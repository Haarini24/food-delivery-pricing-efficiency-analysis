# Food Delivery Pricing & Efficiency Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores food delivery order data to understand pricing patterns, restaurant performance, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

The objective is to identify meaningful business insights and provide data-driven recommendations that could help improve food delivery operations and customer experience.

## Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** Food Ordering and Delivery App Dataset
- **Total Records:** 1,898 orders
- **Total Columns:** 9
- **Restaurants:** 178
- **Data Source:** Hugging Face

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

9. Key Findings
10. Business Recommendations

## Key Findings

- **Weekend Order Volume:** 71.2% of all orders occurred during weekends.
- **Delivery Time:** Average weekday delivery time was 28.34 minutes, compared with 22.47 minutes on weekends.
- **Average Order Value:** The overall average order cost was approximately 16.50.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** The average customer rating was 4.34 out of 5 among rated orders.
- **Missing Feedback:** 736 orders did not contain customer ratings.
- **Restaurant Performance:** Shake Shack recorded the highest order volume, with 219 orders.
- **Customer Ratings and Delivery:** Restaurant-level average ratings and delivery times showed a correlation of -0.343 among restaurants meeting the minimum rating threshold.

## Business Recommendations

1. Improve weekday delivery efficiency by investigating operational delays.
2. Monitor high-volume restaurants to maintain consistent service quality.
3. Identify restaurants with longer delivery times and investigate possible improvement areas.
4. Improve customer feedback collection to reduce missing ratings.
5. Monitor customer satisfaction alongside delivery performance.
6. Plan staffing and delivery capacity based on higher weekend demand.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analysis can help identify patterns in food delivery operations, pricing, restaurant performance, and customer satisfaction.

The findings provide useful insights for operational planning and service improvement. However, the analysis identifies associations rather than proving causation.
